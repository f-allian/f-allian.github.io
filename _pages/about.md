Welcome to my website <span class="wave">👋🏼</span>

I'm currently a Research Software Engineer at <a href="https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">The University of Sheffield</a>. My main responsibility is to develop data and software products into robust research workflows. 

I previously obtained my PhD in applied mathematics where I utilised <a href="https://sdo.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NASA</a> observational data from the Sun to better understand how its atmospheres are heated to multi-million degree temperatures. 
I also hold an MSci in physics with astrophysics from <a href="https://www.gla.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Glasgow University</a>.

I'm passionate about making technical content accessible to everyone.
