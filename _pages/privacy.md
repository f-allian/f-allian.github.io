**Last updated: September 2025**

### What information is collected?

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This collects:

- Pages you visit and how long you spend on them.
- Your approximate location (country and city).
- Device information (phone/computer, browser type, screen size).
- How you found this site (search engine, direct link, social media).
- Which links you click to leave the site.

### Why is this collected?

- To understand which content is useful.
- To improve the website's structure and content.
- To see how people find my work.
- To track the reach of my research and projects.

### Your privacy

- **No personal information**: I don't collect names, email addresses, or any personal details.
- **IP anonymisation**: Google Analytics anonymises your IP address.
- **No selling data**: This data is never sold or shared with third parties.
- **No advertising**: This site doesn't use advertising or marketing trackers.

### Cookies

This site uses cookies (small text files) to:
- Remember if you've visited before.
- Track your journey through the site.
- Count return visits.

You can delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

### Your choices

- **Opt-out**: Install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Block cookies**: Use your browser's privacy settings to block cookies.
- **Browse privately**: Use your browser's private/incognito mode.

### Data retention

- Analytics data is automatically deleted after 14 months.
- No personal data is stored on this website's servers.
